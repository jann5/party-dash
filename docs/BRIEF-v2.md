# Party Dash v2 overhaul: the owner's brief

The owner played the build from the first agent run (review/party-dash @ 62586fa) and wrote this (translated from
Polish, then the original). Every point is a requirement. Reference screenshots of the style they want are in
`docs/reference/ref*.jpg`. `docs/reference/current-game-koth.jpg` shows the current game, which they consider
washed-out, blending, boring, and "AI slop".

## Translation (requirements, in their order)
1. Lots of fixes are needed, especially **bugs** and **looks**.
2. Look: the **simple, blocky, bright Roblox style of the reference screenshots** (that style is popular), but the
   **map, the background and everything must be very, very pretty**.
3. **Controls are very important.** Work on them very carefully and make them 10x better.
4. **Maps**: right now everything blends together too much. They must look like the reference screenshots.
5. **Graphics** (icons/art) are generated with ChatGPT image generation. They must be very good and must match.
6. **Most important**: the game is a bit boring and buggy, and nobody would realistically want to play it. It
   doesn't hook you and doesn't fit the "iPad kid" era. Nothing makes a player play and come back. The modes are not
   dynamic, not engaging, boring. King of the Hill is very weak, because the agents focused on everything and did
   nothing precisely. Now everything must be done **PRECISELY**.
7. Change the colors and the whole visual identity: everything must be 20x better and must not look like AI slop,
   but like a game made by a professional Roblox studio.
8. The game needs **competition, choices**, etc. Think about what a kid (or a slightly older kid) would want to play,
   where they would want to spend money, and what they would want to get for it.
9. The style must be more **modern**.
10. **Replace King of the Hill with BOMB TAG.** Everyone is teleported onto the map and one person spawns with the
    bomb. The bomb has a **10-second fuse**. When the holder explodes, another person gets the bomb. The Bomb Tag map
    must be interesting and dynamic and have obstacles.
11. **Bug: slide** (C or Ctrl): the animation is wrong. They want a **football (soccer) slide-tackle** animation. The
    slide also gets a **cooldown, but no bar** for it.
12. Remember: the game must be very addictive.
13. **Laser Tracer**: the lasers must be **red**, but you must be able to see **which one is the low one and which is the
    high one**.
14. **After a round you get 20 seconds in the lobby**, and only then the roulette (draw) for the next game.
15. **Laser Tracer**: **no pillar in the middle**, and the **lasers must move randomly**.
16. **Bug**: sometimes when they fell into the water after being pushed, they **did not die, they got teleported back**.
17. After dying: an option to **spectate** and an option to **go back to the lobby**.
18. Fix all bugs in general.
19. Make everything pull kids into the game more, so they **come back and spend Robux**.
20. All the UI must look 10x better.
21. **Wall game (Hole in the Wall)**: don't write "RUN" or "JUMP" or "SLIDE" on the walls. Write nothing. Make a
    **simple shape** (hole), but **not always the same one**.
22. **Pillar game (Spin)**: **add a bat**. Sometimes at the bottom of the pillars there is a **small piece of wall you
    can stand on** (exploit, remove it). And on the pillars, **sometimes when they jump the bar still hits them** (fix).
23. **Calm, pleasant background music** that can be **muted in settings**.
24. A **fake loading screen** ("Loading assets..." etc.) with a **Skip** button.
25. **Click sound** on every button; **hit sound**.
26. In the lobby, to take part in a round **you must step into a square** (join zone).
27. A **daily chest** you can claim every day. **Joining the group and liking the game** lets you open an **extra
    chest**.
28. **Revive for Robux** (respawn back into the round).
29. A **wheel of fortune** in the lobby, in the UI, e.g. **9 Robux per spin**.
30. **Starter packs**, e.g. 500 coins.
31. A **limited trail** for **19 Robux**, shown as discounted **from 199**.
32. The **lobby must look 10x better**, and everything in general must be better.
33. Don't ask questions, work through the night.
